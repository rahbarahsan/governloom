# Working together

Keep discussions respectful and focused on the work. Welcome questions and
different experience levels. Critique claims and code with evidence; do not attack
people or make assumptions about their identity.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, impersonation and publication of
another person's private information are unacceptable. Do not post secrets or
customer data. Maintainers may remove content, close discussions or restrict
participation to keep the project usable and respectful.

For sensitive concerns request a private maintainer contact without identifying
details in public. Maintainers assess reports in context; no guaranteed adjudication
is promised. This applies to project issues, pull requests and managed discussions.
