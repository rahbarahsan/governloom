# Releases

## 0.6.0 — discover an application and prepare its monitoring

- Bounded local source/OpenAPI/trace inspection without target execution; optional
  one-request model-assisted analysis with exact source references and provenance.
- Review/correct inferred application behavior and telemetry mappings in the console.
  Preserve immutable evidence, owner confirmations and correction history.
- Prepare frozen calibration/held-out datasets, metrics, budget warnings and
  version-scoped forecast/classification/RAG identity observation proposals.
- Guard policy activation against concurrent requests, replaced policies and newer
  confirmed understandings. Generated rules flag only; enforcement is not enabled.
- Reusable `DiscoveredObserver` applies confirmed mappings while preserving actual
  live versions. Updated GIF shows discovery through an actual forecast incident.

Three owned model-assisted analyses used three bounded subscription requests;
frozen replays made zero. Generated scenarios are unreviewed, and the single RAG
answer remains insufficient for calibration. Independent discovery accuracy and
owner effort are unmeasured. See [evidence](docs/V06_EVIDENCE.md).

## 0.5.0 — outcome coverage, recovery and distribution

- Opt-in numeric outcome deadlines, persisted expectations, bounded restart-safe
  watchdog and scoped coverage console. Late actuals resolve coverage while the
  incident remains available for review.
- Signing-key rotation with overlapping signatures and emergency revocation;
  verified database-plus-key recovery bundles and audited, conservative retention
  of delivered notifications for closed incidents.
- Chronological forecast block gates, grouped vision analysis and 24 frozen
  source-support claims with explicit unknowns. Corrected custom RAG corpus
  retrieval; exact frozen-prompt replay preserves the original capture.
- Standalone fresh-wheel model consumer, non-root container and CI build/restart
  checks. Updated runtime GIF includes real forecast deadline/actual scenes.

The block threshold is empirical under dependence. RAG labels are authored;
the standalone consumer is owned, not independent developer feedback. The 600-event
drill is bounded, not production scale. See [measurements](docs/V05_EVIDENCE.md).
No registry publication or hosted deployment occurred.

## 0.4.0 — incident response and developer onboarding

- Registered host-allowed receivers, Standard Webhooks HMAC signing, transactional
  bodies, restart recovery, bounded retry, visible dead letters and scoped replay.
- Dashboard delivery state, one-time secrets and named-role connection controls.
- One-command actual digit-model quickstart: signed receiver, receipt loss,
  application review/outcome, named mitigation and owned-service cleanup.
- Native async Python/Node hooks, bounded observation batches, explicit event
  preparation, published v1 schema and selected numeric OpenTelemetry span adapter.
- Measured onboarding/overhead, detector misses/false alarms and contributor,
  conduct, issue and security-reporting documentation.

No registry publication or hosted deployment. Operator verification is an
attestation. Semantic labels remain small/authored; production scale, independent
human calibration, SSO/MFA and tenants remain outside demonstrated evidence.
Terminal delivery history needs a storage policy; signing secrets need separate backup.
